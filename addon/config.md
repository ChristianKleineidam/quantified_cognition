* **minimum_reviews**: Below this many evaluated cards, the score remains visible but its label is "insufficient data". Default: 30.
* **minimum_formation_outcome_days**: Formation outcomes must represent at least this many effective Anki outcome days for a range and comparison label. Default: 3. Uneven day sizes reduce effective support: many cards reviewed on one day cannot be made independent by a few cards on other days. Unsupported scores/counts remain visible with an "insufficient data" label and no range.
* **default_display_days**: Number of calendar days in the suggested formation date range. Default: 90, ending 30 calendar days ago. Recall starts with the recent 30 days. Date ranges can be changed in the report; the full history still supplies subsequent outcomes. Older days can still have pending follow-ups.

Use **Update** after changing settings, syncing, or optimizing FSRS. Unchanged analysis is reused; changed calculation inputs trigger a full replay. A fixed CSV export failure leaves saved scores available, and **Retry CSV export** retries only the file step.

**Tools → Quantified Cognition Personal Insights…** uses all saved history by default, including measurements predating installation. Both sleep timings for all three formation metrics are visible alongside recall. It is designed for occasional visits as history grows. Correlations have approximate uncertainty ranges and insufficient-data labels; formation comparisons use study days at least 30 days old with at least 80% evaluated follow-ups. The default removes calendar-month averages. Computation runs in the background and unchanged estimates are reused. **Export insights…** writes a separate summary CSV. See `docs/INSIGHTS.md` for exact timing, eligibility and uncertainty assumptions.

Main sleep and naps are matched using actual session timestamps and the first/last review on each Anki day. **Naps** shows recorded nap days, duration and proximity before/after studying. Recall uses only naps before studying. Duration and proximity use nap days only; missing session coverage is unknown. Exact timestamps stay local; the combined daily CSV and research package contain only the disclosed derived durations, counts, gaps and matching status. Upgrading backfills provider session history once and refreshes Anki anchors once.

Both main sleep and naps show light, deep and REM duration separately, where reported. Nap stages use nap days only, and partial stage coverage stays unknown. Their correlations and ranges describe separate associations; comparing r values alone does not identify a phase's independent importance. Saved sessions already contain the needed stage summaries, so this extension reuses them without FSRS replay or an extra provider backfill.

The default insights overview shows history coverage and three adjusted phase comparisons: light → deep, light → REM and REM → deep, per ten-minute reallocation at fixed total sleep and remaining phase. All three use the same complete-stage days within each memory metric/timing. Formation timing columns remain visible together. **All associations** opens the original correlation tables and scatter plots. Unstable models or sparse history show insufficient data. The personal insights CSV includes both analysis types; fixed daily CSVs and research packages keep their existing disclosed measurements. See `docs/INSIGHTS.md` for the exact model and readiness thresholds.

**Since your last visit** compares newly available history, formation follow-ups on earlier study days, newly eligible formation scores and uncertainty ranges. The baseline advances only after results are displayed. Background updates, exports and tab switches do not consume it, and refreshes keep comparing with the previous visit while the window stays open. The first displayed visit after upgrading establishes a baseline. Changed settings/window/FSRS inputs are flagged before uncertainty is compared. Snapshots stay in the separate profile-local `personal-insights-visits.json`, with only the latest per provider; disconnect/account replacement clears its history. Personal insights exports include comparison fields, while fixed daily/research exports remain unchanged.

Automatic analysis waits until you leave the review screen to copy the collection, then calculates and exports on an independent worker. Automatic CSV retries use saved results and can run during reviewing. Failures retry after one hour without a modal warning; an open report shows the error inline.

Finishing the current review queue requests a fresh update immediately, even if that day's CSV already exists. Closing Anki immediately after the final card preserves the latest reviews before the collection closes. A background worker finishes the analysis and atomic CSV replacement after the window closes, without another click. Only capturing the consistent snapshot needs brief coordination during closing; reviewing never waits for the calculation or export. Unfinished work is kept locally and resumes automatically next opening if an export cannot finish, for example because its destination is locked.

Configure automatic daily updates and the fixed CSV path through **Tools → Quantified Cognition Daily Settings…**, or **Daily settings…** in the memory report. Those preferences are saved separately for each Anki profile. Automatic updates are off until you enable them and choose a CSV file. The results database is saved separately in the profile's `quantified-self-memory/results.sqlite3` folder; manual recalculations save there too.

Retained review logs of deleted cards are analyzed automatically when all current presets agree on active FSRS parameters, historical retention and ignored-history cutoff. Differences in unrelated scheduling preferences do not matter. Otherwise those histories are skipped, with the count and reason available in the report. The add-on creates temporary placeholders only on its disposable copy. Your real cards and presets are unchanged. Method v5 updates formation uncertainty once after upgrading, including when today's old CSV was already complete. Phase comparisons use a finite-block uncertainty floor; see `docs/STATISTICAL_VALIDATION.md` for the reproducible synthetic calibration audit.

Use **Tools → Quantified Cognition Health Data…** and select **Oura** or **Google Health**. Sleep stages and daily steps sync in the background while Anki is open and are stored separately in the profile's `quantified-self-memory/health.sqlite3`. Daily and manual CSV exports combine saved health and Anki measurements. Changed health data refreshes the fixed CSV using saved Anki scores without rerunning FSRS. Provider columns stay separate and are never summed. Oura uses its assigned date; Google sleep uses the local wake date and steps use its civil day. These differ from Anki's rollover. Google imports the past year by default; choose an earlier **Import since** date for older history. Missing stages remain blank, including light/deep/REM for classic sleep. Health sync never submits research data. See `docs/GOOGLE_HEALTH.md` for setup and approval requirements.

`services.base_url` is the operator's public HTTPS service root. This build points to `https://wfjlzecckihsgcvauqwt.supabase.co/functions/v1/qs-service`. The backend is deployed with Oura, Google Health and research uploads disabled until the policy and approvals are ready. Local Anki calculations/CSV work independently. Existing installations can set this public URL through Anki's add-on configuration; no credentials belong there. Never put a provider client secret or Supabase server key in add-on settings. Tokens and upload/deletion credentials use Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain or Linux Secret Service (`secret-tool` required on Linux), with no plaintext fallback.

Research sharing is an optional Yes / Not now / No choice asked before the first report, including cached results, or before enabling automatic CSV output. Change it later via **Tools → Quantified Cognition Research Sharing…**. No registration fields are required. `research_sharing.notice_version` identifies the question's version; an empty `notice_text` uses the built-in question. If the text or version changes, the app asks again without rewriting the original timing audit. Answering Yes does not upload anything.

With Yes selected, reopen Research sharing and choose **Review and submit data…** to inspect the complete package, recipient and privacy policy before sending. Each upload requires an explicit Send. Research submission retries reuse the prepared package ID; they never happen automatically. **Prepare latest data** replaces an unsent package. Not now or No queues deletion of previous uploads, and **Delete uploaded data…** requests deletion separately. Offline deletion retries while Anki is open and blocks new uploads until acknowledged. Oura disconnect deletes local Oura data; research submissions and previously saved CSV files have their own deletion controls.

The v7 sharing choice records a preference and preserves original first-view timing.

eligibility, saveable information and explicit consent linked to the participant ID;

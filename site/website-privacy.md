The [add-on and research privacy policy](/privacy/) covers the desktop app and

website. The business address is Schweidnitzer Straße 6, 10709 Berlin, Germany.
Contact [contact@jollyjoyjourney.com](mailto:contact@jollyjoyjourney.com).

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes request
information, such as IP addresses, browser and request details, and security information,
to deliver and protect the pages. Processing can take place internationally.
Cloudflare's [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
and [data processing information](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/)
describe its handling and safeguards.

We use this processing to provide a reliable, secure information website, based
on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We do not add advertising,
visitor analytics, tracking scripts or application cookies to these pages.
Cloudflare may apply its own security measures. This website has no accounts,
research enrollment or upload forms and collects no Anki or health measurements.

If you contact us, we process the message to handle your request. The

how long it is kept.

You can contact us about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection
or other applicable data-protection rights. You can also complain to a data
protection authority, including the
[Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information](https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de/ueber-uns/kontakt).

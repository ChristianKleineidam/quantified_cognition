01 THE MEASUREMENT

See how effectively
you learned each day.

Anki can tell you how many cards you reviewed and how often you answered correctly. Quantified Cognition adds an estimate of memory formation on the day you studied, using what happened when those cards were reviewed later.

Later answers are compared with Anki’s FSRS predictions, accounting for differences between cards and the time until their next review. The result is assigned back to the earlier study day. Daily recall scores describe how you performed when answering.

Formation is shown overall and separately for follow-ups within 30 days and beyond 30 days. Scores include review counts, uncertainty and follow-up coverage. They mature as more cards are reviewed again; missing follow-ups are not treated as failures.

FOR YOUR OWN QUESTIONS

Explore changes in
your learning performance.

Compare periods in your life, study routines or personal experiments—including whether a supplement coincides with a change in learning performance. Start with the history you already have, then return after more outcomes have accumulated.

Review counts, uncertainty and follow-up coverage help you judge how much evidence supports a pattern. The Methods page explains how to interpret the comparisons.

SLEEP & ACTIVITY

Sleep stages, naps and activity.

The Oura integration brings sleep stages and daily steps alongside your Anki measurements. Explore deep, REM and light sleep, the nights before and after studying, and naps separately. Phase comparisons account for total sleep duration. Oura workouts add exercise duration, intensity and timing relative to studying.

LEARNING ABOUT LEARNING

Your observations.
A wider scientific question.

Your everyday studying can contribute to research on how learning varies. The programme explores relationships with sleep, activity and study patterns, and develops and tests methods for measuring these changes. When research sharing opens, you can choose to contribute a package of daily measurements and pseudonymous review predictions and outcomes after reviewing it. Card content, original Anki IDs and account credentials are excluded.

Personal use is independent of research participation.

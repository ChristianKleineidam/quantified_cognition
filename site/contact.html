Current status

The desktop add-on is in beta. The project’s purpose, measurements and proposed research are described on this site. Oura and Google Health connections, research enrollment and research uploads are currently closed while reviews and operational checks are completed.

Personal Anki analysis and CSV export work independently of research participation. This website has no research enrollment or upload form.

About data requests

Please use the add-on’s research deletion controls when available. If you need help, contact us with your saved receipt. Please do not email Anki collections, sleep exports, account credentials or other health records.
